# Visuels du mur

Générés avec l’outil intégré ImageGen, puis copiés dans le catalogue du projet. Ces photos sont fictives et servent à la démonstration. Aucune dépendance à une URL externe.

Fichiers :

## Prompts utilisés

**StoneWall**

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background texture for a playful French iPhone app wall of shame. Primary request: a straight-on tightly framed warm beige limestone masonry wall, irregular rectangular stone blocks, fine real stone grain, soft mortar joints, natural diffuse daylight, subtle warm shadows. Full frame wall only, flat front view, no perspective, no floor, no objects, no text, no people, no plants, no vignette. Portrait composition. Light enough to display pinned photo cards over it.

**ProofCostume**

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional demo proof photo for a friendly student walking challenge app. Primary request: candid phone photograph of an adult university student wearing a silly bright yellow banana costume over normal clothes, smiling and waving on a leafy French university campus. Friendly harmless humorous dare. Medium full body portrait, warm natural daylight, slightly imperfect authentic phone camera snapshot, no text, no brands, no watermark. Portrait composition.

**ProofDance**

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: fictional demo proof photo for a friendly student walking challenge app. Primary request: candid phone photograph of an adult university student doing a funny joyful little dance wearing a pink bucket hat and casual purple sweatshirt, outside on a leafy university campus. Friendly harmless humorous dare. Full body portrait, warm natural daylight, authentic phone camera snapshot, no text, no brands, no watermark. Portrait composition.
